Design for accessibility

Custom cardboard chair for a preschooler

I designed a chair for a preschooler with cerebral palsy, who often fell out of his seat because of low muscle tone. The sides are laser cut from triwall cardboard. I cut the seat and backrest section on a table saw, then finished it by hand with a blade. The chair has proper arm supports, a sliding foot stool and a storage shelf under the seat.

Metal casting

Sand casting a dog-bone specimen

I sand cast a 6061 aluminum dog-bone specimen, then tensile tested it to failure on an Instron at 5 mm/min. It reached a peak force of 8.90 kN and fractured suddenly at about 1.9% strain.
